FLASH8 — SPIDriver Quattro / EVE flash validation fixture

DESIGN
All resistors, capacitors and LEDs use imperial 0402 footprints.
49 x 49 mm, four layers: top signals / GND / 3.3 V / bottom signals.
Trace width and copper clearance: 0.100 mm. Hex grid row spacing: 0.3750 mm.
Via drill: 0.200 mm; via copper pad: 0.450 mm. Vias may not occupy adjacent
hex cells. The grid is exported as flash8.HEX.
The original SPIDriver Quattro mating header locations are preserved.
J1, north to south: GND, GND, 3.3 V, 3.3 V, 5 V, 5 V.
J2, north to south: SCLK, MOSI/IO0, MISO/IO1, IO2, IO3, CS, A, B.
The two 5 V header pins are not connected to the fixture electronics.
Supply the fixture only with 3.3 V logic and 3.3 V-compatible flash devices.

U1: SN74LVC595APWR, TSSOP-16 (TI PW0016A).
  Center: (8.8, 26.0) mm, rotated 180 degrees with QB-QH facing east.
  C1 follows VCC to the southwest; R1 sits west of OE with its signal
  pad facing the IC. Their power connections follow the new placement.
  14 SER   <- A
  11 SRCLK <- B
  12 RCLK  <- B (same net as SRCLK)
  13 /OE   <- host /CS, with 10 kohm pull-up to 3.3 V
  10 /SRCLR and 16 VCC -> 3.3 V
   8 GND -> ground
   9 QH' -> unconnected
  A has a 10 kohm pull-up; B has a 10 kohm pull-down.
  R1 sits adjacent to U1's OE pin, with its signal pad facing U1.
  R2-R5 sit beside the J2 pins for A, B, IO2 and IO3 respectively.

Slots F0..F3 are the left column, bottom to top. F4..F7 are the right column.

Eight soldered SOIC-8-208mil footprints, 5.3 mm wide body, 1.27 mm pitch.
These are bare IC footprints, not ZIF socket footprints.
Flash pinout: 1 /CS, 2 MISO/IO1, 3 /WP/IO2, 4 GND,
              5 MOSI/IO0, 6 SCLK, 7 /HOLD or /RESET/IO3, 8 VCC.
Confirm the selected flash is pin-compatible and 3.3 V before population.

Each CS has a 470 ohm pull-up to 3.3 V. Each indicator branch is:
  3.3 V -> 1.5 kohm -> red LED anode -> LED cathode -> CSn.
D1..D8 correspond to F0..F7. The silk bar marks each LED cathode.
Each LED and its series resistor sit north of that flash's CS pull-up and
decoupling capacitor, grouping the chip-select connections locally.
Each pair is rotated 180 degrees: LED west with its cathode toward the CS
pull-up, and series resistor east with its supply pad facing east.
The selected output sinks about 7 mA through its pull-up plus approximately
0.9 mA through a red LED with 2 V forward voltage. Brightness depends on LED.
The LED follows the active-low transaction interval, not flash busy time;
very short transactions may not produce a visibly bright indication.

C1: 100 nF adjacent to U1 VCC; C2: 10 uF bulk adjacent to J1's 3.3 V pins.
C3..C10: 100 nF, one per flash. Use ceramic capacitors rated at least 6.3 V;
C2 is specified as 10 V X5R. Inner layer 2 is GND; inner layer 3 is 3.3 V.
IO2 and IO3 connect to every flash and J2, with one 10 kohm pull-up each.
They remain available for QSPI; no hard ties to a supply are present.
There are no dedicated test points. Bus and control signals are accessible
at the mating header.

SELECTION PROTOCOL
1. Drive host CS high. All QA..QH outputs are high-impedance, all flash
   chip selects rise through their 470 ohm pull-ups, and all LEDs are off.
2. Drive B low. For slot n, form pattern 0xFF XOR (1 << n).
3. Send bits 7..0 on A. For every bit, set A, then pulse B high and low.
4. Send a ninth B pulse (A can be high). With SRCLK and RCLK tied together,
   the latch is one clock behind the shift register; this pulse is required.
5. Keep B low during transactions. Drive host CS low for a transaction,
   transfer SPI/QSPI data, then drive host CS high to deselect.
6. Before enabling outputs after startup, explicitly program a valid pattern.
   Program 0xFF plus the ninth pulse to put the fixture into all-off state.
Never clock A/B selection while CS is low: intermediate patterns can select
multiple devices and cause contention on the shared bus.

SOFTWARE
flash8_control.py provides Flash8.program(slot), Flash8.transaction(slot),
and Flash8.jedec_id(slot). It programs the nine-clock sequence with CS high,
rejects nested transactions, and deselects on transfer exceptions.
Run with the project Python:
  /Users/jamesb/.pyenv/versions/3.13.0/envs/py13/bin/python flash8_control.py SERIAL_PORT
The CLI reads JEDEC IDs only, uses SPI mode 0, and sets Quattro to 1 MHz.
It does not erase, program, reset, or change protection/QE bits in the flash.
Devices already in QPI, deep power-down, or another persistent mode may need
part-specific recovery before the ID command works. QSPI command sequences,
QE handling and EVE-specific compatibility checks remain device-specific.

VALIDATION AND USE
Generate: python flash8.py
Check generated copper: python -m tools.check_flash8
Control tests: python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_flash8_control.py
Use the Python interpreter specified in AGENTS.md for these commands.
The check report is flash8-checks.json. It checks physical connectivity from
Gerbers/drills, independent pin mappings, copper/edge clearance and drills.
The control tests model the shared-clock latch independently of the helper.

No physical prototype has been tested. Start slowly, then verify SCLK, data
and CS edges at the farthest flash before increasing speed. Eight input loads,
branches and probe leads limit usable speed; no maximum QSPI rate is claimed.
Check the Quattro 3.3 V supply budget for the actual population and operating
mode. Use one bus master at a time if attaching another host for EVE testing.
Generic flash, passive and LED BOM entries require exact procurement choices.
The board has not been released for manufacture: package/polarity, assembly,
mask/paste and manual CAM review remain necessary before ordering.
